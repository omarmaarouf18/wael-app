## Data safety — what the code actually collects

Server account data (`services/auth-service/internal/models/models.go`):
`full_name`, `email`, `phone` (normalized E.164). Email carries the OTP and
account notices; name and phone identify the student and watermark the video
player (falls back to email when empty).

Device identity: `device_id` (random UUIDv4 generated by the app on first
launch, kept in secure storage, sent on login/OTP) with optional
`device_label` (≤64 runes). Stored in server `sessions`; no fingerprinting, no
IP. Reinstall = new device.

Activity: `video_plays` (`user_id`, `video_id`, `subject_id`, `played_at`) is
appended on every video play (no IP). `purchase_requests`, `entitlements` and
append-only `payment_records` hold (`user_id`, `subject_id`, status, price at
grant). `account_events` holds (`user_id`, type, `created_at`) with no PII.
Notifications hold `user_id` with optional `subject_id`.

On-device only (never uploaded): catalog cache (levels/subjects/entitlements
JSON in secure storage, cleared on logout), downloaded PDFs (app-private
cache, deleted on sign-out, account switch, or loss of ownership), installed
app version (read locally for update gating).

Shared only on tap: the support WhatsApp number (opens `https://wa.me` in the
external app) and the center map URL (external map). The app collects no
contacts, no SMS, no location, no health or financial data. Payment happens
outside the app; the app has no payment SDK and no ads SDK (`pubspec.yaml`).

Data is transmitted over HTTPS, stored encrypted at rest server-side
(AES-256-GCM for files; password hashes never leave the server), and student
API responses are DTOs that never include password hashes, OTPs, tokens, or
YouTube ids.

## Account deletion (store requirement)

- In-app: Settings → delete account. The student confirms with the password
  and by typing حذف (or DELETE in English UI); the account enters a 30-day
  grace period (`pending_deletion`) and signing in cancels it. After the purge
  the profile is anonymized and the email/phone may register again.
- Web: `https://legal.elmetracademy.app/delete-account` (always available;
  there is no app-config field for it, the app always uses this URL).
  The delete-account screen links to it.

## Privacy / terms URLs

- Served by `GET /academy/app-config` (`TERMS_URL` / `PRIVACY_URL`, `https://`
  required outside dev), with always-working bundled fallbacks
  (`frontend/lib/core/legal_links.dart`): `https://legal.elmetracademy.app/terms`
  and `https://legal.elmetracademy.app/privacy`. English UI appends `#en`.
- The signup consent checkbox opens an in-app summary sheet with full-page
  links; the box stays disabled until ticked.

## Consumption-only rule and banned wording

Content may be bought outside the app, but the app must not lead users to an
outside payment method: no Play Billing, no pay/buy/purchase/refund wording,
no payment methods, no payment links, no payment-amount leadership — prices
may be shown, payment actions never (SPEC Section 2 D3, ADR-0003 amendment
2026-10-08). The guard is `frontend/test/store_safety_l10n_test.dart`: it
scans `lib/` for payment-action words (Arabic substring, Latin loose +
word-boundary lists) with an allowlist limited to the price label/helper/wire
keys. Keep every new string passing it before release.
